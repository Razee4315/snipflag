Snipflag's first v1 preview: capture, annotate, and turn multiple screenshots into one Linear issue.

## What's included

- Compact frameless workspace with daylight and dark themes, minimal controls, and a new abstract S icon.
- Multiple screenshots, independent annotations and undo histories, reorder, captions, clipboard and PNG export.
- Shift-constrained arrows and pen segments at 15° increments; Shift rectangles draw squares.
- Description image mentions: type `@` to choose a thumbnail, filter by caption, and insert with Enter or Tab. References keep their identity after reorder and become clickable image links in Linear.
- Local draft recovery, settings sections, configurable capture shortcut, history retention, and a QA reproduction template.
- Linear OAuth, ordered flattened images, and stable issue identity with reconciliation on uncertain retries.

## Downloads

Choose the installer for your platform below. Windows x64 offers `.exe` and `.msi`; macOS offers separate Apple Silicon and Intel `.dmg` files; Linux x64 offers `.deb` and `.AppImage`. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists checksums for every installer.

**Unsigned prerelease:** Windows/macOS signing and notarization are not configured. Native capture, mixed-DPI window behavior, platform integrations, and real Linear image-reference rendering still require the documented runtime acceptance checks. Automated checks and packaging do not establish those results. This release is not marked stable.

Screenshots and setup instructions are in the [README](https://github.com/Razee4315/snipflag#screenshots). Connect your Linear OAuth client ID in Settings; screenshots remain local until you explicitly create an issue.
