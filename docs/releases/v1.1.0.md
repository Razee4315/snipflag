Snipflag 1.1.0: new annotation tools, a custom color picker, a full-size workspace, and a more polished desktop feel.

## New

- **Numbered steps (N):** badges that count up per screenshot, readable on any color.
- **Ellipse (E):** hold Shift for a circle.
- **Highlighter (H):** marker inks painted beneath your other marks.
- **Custom colors:** color field, hue slider and hex entry. Your last five colors are remembered.
- **Sounds:** soft synthesized cues for captures, added images, created issues and errors. You can switch them off in Settings.
- **About:** Settings → About links to the creator, the source code and the license.

## Improved

- The editor opens at full workspace size, and very large captures maximize the window.
- Pixelate averages real color blocks (at least 12 px) and is burned into every export. It is now the privacy tool; the solid redaction tool was removed, but older drafts still show their redactions.
- Smoother pen and highlighter strokes, a brush-size cursor, and an 8 px default stroke.
- Redrawn tool icons, themed pickers, switches and scrollbars, and a redesigned utility bar and empty state for light and dark themes.
- Browser-style shortcuts and menus (reload, print, downloads, devtools) are blocked, so the app behaves like a desktop app.
- Faster capture overlays and startup.

## Downloads

Choose the installer for your platform below. Windows x64 offers `.exe` and `.msi`, macOS offers separate Apple silicon and Intel `.dmg` files, and Linux x64 offers `.deb` and `.AppImage`. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists a checksum for every installer.

**Unsigned installers:** Windows and macOS signing and notarization are not configured yet, so SmartScreen and Gatekeeper will ask before opening the app. On macOS, allow Screen Recording when asked. Automated checks and packaging passed on all platforms; native runtime acceptance checks (multi-monitor and mixed-DPI capture, window behavior, sounds in the desktop webview) are still being completed. Published as the latest release at the owner's explicit request.

The demo and setup instructions are in the [README](https://github.com/Razee4315/snipflag#see-it-in-action) and on the [website](https://razee4315.github.io/snipflag/). Add your Linear OAuth client ID in Settings. Screenshots stay on your computer until you click Create issue.
