# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Snipflag is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and ways of working. We want taking part in this project to be respectful and productive for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with people who are new to the project.
- Keep discussion focused on the work: the problem, the evidence, and the trade-offs.
- Give feedback on ideas and code, not on the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Credit the work of others.
- Respect privacy. Do not post screenshots, logs, or data that contain someone else's personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Sexualized language or content, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or project spaces.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately using the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([@Razee4315](https://github.com/Razee4315)). Include links or screenshots where possible. Reports are handled confidentially, and nobody will face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and may respond, depending on severity, with:

1. **A private note** explaining why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **A warning** with clear expectations for future conduct.
3. **A temporary ban** from project spaces.
4. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this code.
